Product Assumptions – FindIt

1. What product are you planning to build?
Explain what FindIt is and what students can do with it.

2. What problem does it solve?
Describe the difficulties students face when they lose personal belongings on campus.

3. Who are the target users?
Mention the students and other people who may use the application.

4. What is the main use case?
Describe what happens when a student loses an item and wants to report it.

5. What are the core features?
List the main features currently planned for FindIt.

 6. What assumptions are you making about users?
Write what you expect about students' willingness to report lost items and check reported items online.

 7. What will be included in the MVP?
Mention the basic features you plan to deliver in the first version.

 8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?
Mention features you are postponing, such as live notifications, GPS tracking, and online chat.
